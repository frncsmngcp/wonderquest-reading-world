📖 How to Play

1
Choose Easy, Medium, or Difficult.
All three levels are made for Grade 1 learners.
2
Press Start Today's Word.
The app gives one curated word for the day.
3
Read the word, meaning, and sentence.
Press the purple speaker to hear them aloud.
4
Use Next Word or Shuffle.
Shuffle randomly picks another word from the selected level.
5
Build My AWAD.
Teachers can add, edit, and use up to 100 custom words.

Keyboard: F = Fullscreen, Space = Say It, → = Next Word, ← = Previous Word, S = Shuffle, H = Home.
