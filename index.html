Teacher Francis Alex T. Mongcopa is a Grade 1 teacher at the Schools Division Office – City of Malolos who is passionate about helping young learners discover the joy of reading. He believes that learning to read becomes more meaningful when children are given engaging, interactive, and enjoyable experiences.

Driven by his love for teaching and educational technology, he developed WonderQuest: Reading World to provide young readers with fun and accessible activities that strengthen foundational reading skills. The project was created not only for his own learners, but also as a resource that fellow teachers and parents can use to support children as they grow from early letter recognition to becoming confident readers.
